CouchCare Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

CouchCare is published by Northglass Labs. It provides personal guides for Android TV, phones and tablets.

Data collection

CouchCare does not collect or share data with its developer. It has no account, advertising, analytics or tracking. The app does not have permission to connect to the internet.

Data stored on your device

Guides you create or edit are saved in the app’s private storage. They can include text and the package name of an app you choose for a shortcut. Anyone who can use the app on your device can read or edit them. Do not put passwords or sensitive information in guides.

Device information and apps

CouchCare reads network connection status, available internal storage, media volume and apps with launchable activities. This information is used on the device to show status and let you select shortcuts. It is not sent to the developer.

Import and backup

You can export a guide file to a location you select with Android’s file picker, or import a file you select. A cloud storage provider you choose may upload that file under its own privacy policy. CouchCare does not automatically upload it. Automatic cloud backup and device transfer are excluded.

Other apps

A shortcut can open Android settings or another installed app. That app’s own privacy policy applies after you open it.

Deleting your information

Delete a guide in Edit guides. Clearing CouchCare’s app data or uninstalling it removes its local guides. Exported files remain where you saved them; delete those separately using your file provider.
